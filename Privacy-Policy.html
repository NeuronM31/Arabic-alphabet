Privacy Policy

This app collects no personal data, sends nothing to any server of ours, and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking. Everything created while using the app stays on your device.

01Who is responsible

The app “M31 Edu Arabic Alphabet” (identifier ch.m31.edu.arabic) is published by Neuron M31 (Andrzej Węgrzyn). For privacy matters, write to the address given at the top of this page.

02Permissions

The app does not ask for access to your microphone, camera, contacts, photos, files or location. On Android it uses only the standard permissions needed for in-app purchases and an internet connection (com.android.vending.BILLING, INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE). Details of its network use are in section 05.

03Data stored on your device

The app stores the following on your device only. We have no access to it.

  • the interface language and appearance (light or dark),
  • a first name, only if you choose to enter one — it is optional; without it the app shows “Learner”,
  • an installation code made from the language code and the date and time of first setup, shown in “About”,
  • your progress — which letters have been passed (stored in encrypted form),
  • whether Premium has been purchased.

What you write with your finger in Calligraphy and in the Tests is processed only in the device’s memory to work out the match percentage. It is not saved and not sent anywhere. Test results are not saved either.

If you have Android Backup (Auto Backup) enabled, Android may copy these data to your Google account; on iOS the same can happen through an iCloud backup of your device. This is what lets your progress return after reinstalling the app or moving to a new phone. It happens within Google’s or Apple’s services, under their privacy policies, and remains under your control — you can turn it off in your device settings.

Uninstalling the app deletes the stored data. You can also reset it by clearing the app’s data in system settings. Backups are managed in your Google or Apple account settings.

04Letter pronunciation

The app reads letter names aloud with your device’s built-in text-to-speech engine (for example Google Speech Services on Android or the system voice on iOS). Only the name of the letter, written in Arabic, is passed to that engine. The engine is part of the operating system and works under its provider’s privacy terms.

The app does not record sound and does not use the microphone. If a future version adds pronunciation checking, which would need the microphone, we will update this policy before that version is released, and the app will ask for permission first.

05In-app purchases and internet connection

Paid features (Premium) are sold through the billing system of Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iOS). The store handles the entire purchase process — the app never sees or stores card details, billing addresses or any other payment information. It receives only a confirmation that a purchase is active, which is stored on the device. Parents can require approval for purchases in the store (Google Family Link, Apple “Ask to Buy”).

The app connects to the internet only to:

  • handle purchases through the store,
  • check whether a newer version is available — on Android through Google Play’s in-app update service, on iOS through a request to Apple’s App Store lookup service that contains only the app’s identifier and your device’s region code (country),
  • open the links you choose: “Contact”, the licence and the store page open in your browser or store app; “Recommend me” opens your device’s share sheet with a link to the app — you choose the app and the recipient.

It makes no other connections.

06For parents and children

M31 Edu Arabic Alphabet is designed for children aged about 4–8 and for adults beginning to learn the Arabic alphabet. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children — or from anyone. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no chat and no user-submitted content.

Before a purchase, before any link that leaves the app, before sharing and before opening system settings, the app shows a parental gate: a short multiplication task written in words, meant to be solved by an adult.

07What the app does not do

  • no user account, no sign-in,
  • collects no email address, phone number, advertising identifier or device identifier,
  • does not determine or store your location,
  • does not record sound, take photos or read your files,
  • uses no analytics, usage statistics, crash-reporting or tracking services,
  • shows no advertising,
  • has no chat, no user-submitted content and no leaderboards,
  • shares no data with third parties, because it gathers none.

08Your rights

Because we neither collect nor process your personal data, we hold no information that we could disclose, correct or delete on request. You control the data stored by the app yourself, through your device settings. If you have any privacy questions, write to the contact address.

09Changes to this policy

If the app’s behaviour changes in the future, we will update this page and change the “Valid from” date at the top. We recommend checking this page occasionally.

04Wymowa liter

Aplikacja odczytuje nazwy liter wbudowanym w urządzenie syntezatorem mowy (na przykład Usługi mowy Google na Androidzie albo głos systemowy w iOS).
